ABOUT MEESHO
Meesho is an Indian e-commerce platform where customers can buy products from various sellers.

PAYMENT OPTIONS
- Meesho supports prepaid payments such as UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on selected products depending on seller and delivery location.
- Meesho currently does NOT provide Pay Later or EMI options.

ORDER & DELIVERY
- Orders are usually delivered within 5–10 business days.
- Delivery time may vary depending on seller, courier partner, and customer location.
- Delays may occur due to logistics or seller-related issues.

RETURNS & REFUNDS
- Most products are eligible for return within 7 days of delivery.
- Return eligibility depends on product category and seller policy.
- Refunds are processed after successful pickup and quality check.
- Refunds are credited to the original payment method.

ORDER ISSUES
- If an order is delayed, customers are advised to wait for the expected delivery date.
- If an order is not delivered, customers can contact support for assistance.
- If a product is damaged or incorrect, customers can request a return or replacement.

GENERAL SUPPORT BEHAVIOR
- Always respond politely and clearly.
- Explain policies in simple language.
- If exact information is unavailable, say so politely and guide the user.
